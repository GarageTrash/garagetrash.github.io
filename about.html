About the label

Made for the
back of the room.

02

Garage Trash is an independent record label rooted in Oklahoma punk.

We started with a simple idea: document the bands doing the work and get their music onto physical records.

No polish for polish’s sake. No pretending the scene is somewhere else. Just honest releases from loud people in overlooked places.

01

Local first

Oklahoma bands, artists, venues, and scenes deserve a permanent record.

02

Physical always

Music should live beyond a feed. We believe in vinyl, sleeves, inserts, and liner notes.

03

Do it together

Good records come from a community, not a boardroom.
